About me

Portrait photo here

I wanted to be a cricketer. An injury ended the trials, so I gave everything to dance.

I’m John Cole, a choreographer, performer, MC and creative director from Harare. I started in 2004 with the Slicker dance crew and have been moving Zimbabwe ever since: music videos for the biggest artistes, stages, TV, and over 500 weddings.

I founded the Zimbabwe Choreographers and Dancers Association, and I love teaching and lifting the next generation of dancers and creatives.
